* **Parameters:**

**2. Tiered BTC-Drawdown Risk Scaling (Filter / Sizing)**
*Rationale:* Previous learnings rejected hard stop-loss caps and aggressive trailing stops, but validated "gently reducing risk" during specific macro dips (-1% to -3%). This proposes a dynamic risk multiplier rather than a hard exit, preserving win rates while buffering drawdowns.
* **Indicators:** BTC 24-Hour Return (from high).
* **Logic:** Dynamically scale the strategy's base position size or risk limit based on BTC's immediate retracement depth.
* **Parameters:**

  * **Strict Defense:** If `BTC_24h_Return <= -3.0%` → `Risk_Multiplier = 0.5` (Preserve capital, aligning with the accepted 0.5 down-trend risk exposure).
